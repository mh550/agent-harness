# A claim that never reached the list is named, retried once told, and given up with its reason

*(2026-11-09, one `claude` session on the spec `docs/specs/2026-11-09-silent-unpass-fix.md`, milestone G1; the case:
A4-01 in run `agent-harness-2026-11-07-3` (its record under `.harness/runs/agent-harness-2026-11-07-3/`: `harness-log.md`
20:42–20:45, `entries.jsonl` session `15-A4-01`, `needs.md`'s `given_up` card), diagnosed in
`findings-and-research/2026-11-09-silent-unpass-diagnosis.md` (its §2 table is the session-end path, §4 the stub traces);
the retro proposal `verify-failed-recheck` of that run, answered by the finding's baseline trace and closed with it.)*

- **Finding:** a coding session's verdict is the `passes` key of its copy of `feature_list.json` (`lib/lane.sh`, the
  gate and the `passes=` line), and nothing else. The verify's re-check had un-passed A4-01 and granted it one attempt;
  the retry fixed the finding, committed, wrote `outcome: passes` in its entry and said "A4-01 passes" in its final
  message, and never wrote the key. The driver had the entry's word in hand four lines earlier (the ingest) and compared
  it with nothing, so the session read as "not yet" with no note; the `false` spent the granted attempt, `schedule` gave
  the feature up, `blockedLines` had no dependant to name, the lane closed `given up (nothing left to try)`, and the
  `given_up` card's fixed why ("Its sessions' entries say what failed") pointed at two entries that both say passes. A
  $57 run ended `no feature left to try` on one missing JSON key. Both rules of the effort were broken: a guard names
  what it found; a recoverable gap gets one told retry before any card.
- **Choice:**
  - **The gap is named** (G1-01). `claimGap({ id, outcome, passes })` in `lib/lane.mjs` gives the note's words only
    for `outcome === "passes"` with `passes === false`: `<id>: its entry says passes but feature_list.json's passes
    stayed false; the claim never reached the list (the flip is the session's one list edit, prompts/coding.md §3.1)`
    (*default* words). The lane asks it after `F repair` and before the gate, writes the words with `note` and, the
    first time for the feature in the run, to `$STATE/claim-gap-<id>`. The entry's outcome comes from the ingest:
    `entries.mjs ingest` prints one more labelled line, `outcome <word>`, and `journal_` keeps it in
    `$LS/outcome-<id>` (*default*: one file per feature, where the spec said `$LS/outcome`; a lane with two given-up
    features would otherwise name the last session's word for both). A minimal entry's outcome is the driver's own
    (`passes` only when the copy's key says so), so it opens no gap; `not yet` or `parked` with the key `false`, and
    anything with the key `true`, is no gap: the gate's notes speak for those. The list stays the verdict: nothing
    reads a claim as a pass.
  - **One told retry** (G1-02). When the gap is the feature's first in the run (`claim-gap-<id>` did not exist) and no
    attempt is left, the lane grants one (`record F grant`, the re-check's mechanism) and notes `<id>: one more attempt,
    told to set passes (once per run)`; with attempts left nothing is granted, but the file is written, so the next
    session is told. The next coding session of the feature gets the words: `lib/lane.sh` passes `HARNESS_GAP` from the
    file's first line, `lib/prompt.sh` fills `{{GAP}}` for the coding template alone, and `prompts/coding.md` has under
    *This session* (never above `{{SECTIONS}}`) a `{{?GAP}}## The session before this one` block: the words, then
    "Check every step holds on this tree, run the fast tier once, and set `"passes": true` on your feature in
    `feature_list.json`: that key is the one thing the harness reads. Build nothing new." After that session ends,
    whatever its verdict, the file gains a second line `told` (*default*: the words stay as line 1 and `told` is a line
    after them, where the spec said the content becomes `told`; the lane's close and the card need the words after the
    retry too). A second gap of the same feature in the run grants nothing and tells nothing more: once per feature per
    run, as `review-escalated-<id>`; `harness continue` leaves both files, as it leaves that one.
  - **The give-up names its reason** (G1-03). `givenUpLines(fs, att, max, parked, lane)` in `lib/features.mjs` (the
    subcommand `given-up`, the mirror of `blockedLines`) lists the lane's given-up features as `<id>\t<attempts>`:
    attempts spent, not passing, not parked (a parked one is the lane's `parked <ids>`). The lane closes `given up
    (<id> after <n> attempts: <last verdict>[; <id2> …])`, the last verdict the claim-gap file's first clause (its
    `<id>: ` prefix dropped) when the file exists, else `its last entry: <outcome>` from `outcome-<id>`; `given up
    <ids>` and `given up (nothing left to try)` remain for a lane with no given-up feature of its own. `<n>` is
    `attempts.json`'s count, the card's word (*default*: the spec's check text said `3 attempts` for three sessions,
    but a granted attempt lowers the counter by one, so `attempts` means the counter everywhere, as in the card's
    headline `given up after 2 attempts`; the sessions are in `sessions.log` and `metrics.jsonl`). The `given_up` card
    gets `gap=` (the file, whole) and `recheck=1` when `$STATE/recheck-<id>` exists (the verify's `fail` branch touches
    it beside its `F grant`); `lib/needs.mjs` ends the why with "The verify's re-check sent it back (`verify <m>: <id>
    failed the re-check`), and " when the re-check is set, then the gap words (with "; its told retry did not set it
    either" when the file has its `told` line), else "its sessions' entries say what failed"; the old sentence stands
    alone only when neither is set.
  - **The stub mirrors the session** (G1-04). `STUB_NO_FLIP=<id>:<n>[,…]` in `tests/stub-claude.sh`: that coding
    session writes its entry with `outcome: passes` and ends `<id> passes. The commit is pushed.` without `set_passes`:
    the finding's two-line change, upstreamed; the only stub change.
  - Checks: three unit tests on `claimGap`, one on `givenUpLines`, one on the card's why; `tests/checks/claim-gap.sh`
    (fast: two `demo` runs, ~15 s) with five checks: the note between the second `A: suite ran` and `passes=false`, the
    told retry's prompt, the round-2 verify and merge, the lane's close words and the card's why when the told retry
    gaps too. Ten names, none removed.
- **Rejected:**
  - *The entry's `passes` taken as the flip.* The list is the guard's one input (D-H3, D-H4); a session that cannot
    write one key has not shown the steps hold, and reading the entry as the verdict would let the next session's
    "passes" with nothing done through.
  - *A stronger model for the retry.* A format gap, not a judgment gap (rule ii of the effort): the told retry runs on
    the feature's own model with the words in its prompt.
  - *Prose matching of the final message.* The entry is the session's structured word and the prompt makes `outcome`
    required; "A4-01 passes" in prose is read by nothing.
  - *Re-verifying only the failed steps* (the retro proposal `verify-failed-recheck`): the finding's baseline trace
    shows the lane re-verifying (round 2) and merging as soon as the retry's flip reaches the list; the lane closed
    because the flip never did. Closed against the finding.
